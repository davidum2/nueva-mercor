### Key Details
- **Configuration:** Sets up Cloud Storage input and output locations, configures de-identification templates for unstructured data, structured data (`CSV`), and images, and connects a BigQuery table for transformation details.
- **Job Submission & Polling:** Submits the de-identification inspection job via `createDlpJob` and polls `getDlpJob` until the job finishes.
- **Summary Reporting:** Prints the job name, final status, and a list of all detected sensitive information types along with their occurrence counts.
